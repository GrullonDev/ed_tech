## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a calm, focused, and emotionally reassuring atmosphere tailored for mutual accountability and consistent micro-habits. Rather than adopting the hyper-gamified, anxious patterns common in modern productivity apps, the visual aesthetic prioritizes psychological safety, quiet discipline, and warm communal support.

### Emotional Palette & Narrative
- **Composed Confidence:** Interactions feel deliberate and grounded; growth is treated as an organic, day-by-day practice.
- **Intimate Camaraderie:** Small, trusted circles (6–8 members) call for an interface that feels personal, cozy, and humane rather than algorithmic.
- **Supportive Spark:** Momentum and progress celebrations glow with warmth rather than jarring alarms or aggressive dopamine spikes.

### Design Movement
The interface operates under a refined **Warm Minimalist Tactile** philosophy. It merges strict spatial minimalism (abundant white space, purposeful layout discipline, and uncluttered content hierarchies) with soft, organic depth—gentle surface transitions, pill-shaped tactile controls, and subtle radiant glows that honor personal and collective consistency.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around growth, warmth, and balanced clarity. 

- **Primary (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Represents steady habit growth, mindfulness, and tranquil execution. Used for core affirmative actions, progress tracks, active habit completion states, and navigational anchors.
- **Secondary (`#F97316` / `#F59E0B`):** Represents vital social warmth and consistency fire. Dedicated strictly to streak counters, milestone embers, momentum badges, and collective encouragement accents.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981`):** A luminous mint-emerald tint used for success micro-interactions, subtle tinted backdrops for completed states, and organic graph fills.
- **Neutral Palette (`#0F172A` Slate Core):** Text and structural boundaries use balanced slate variations instead of raw black, ensuring optical warmth. Light mode utilizes `#F8FAFC` as an ambient canvas with pure `#FFFFFF` for elevated structural cards. Dark mode maps onto deep slate layers (`#0B0F19`, `#1E293B`) maintaining equal luminance contrast without harsh OLED clipping.

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans** to maintain geometric clarity infused with friendly, contemporary curves. The system balances structural readability with energetic numeric display:

- **Display & Large Headlines:** Used sparingly for daily completion summaries, streak milestones, and circle entry headings. Tighter tracking creates an authoritative, modern feel.
- **Body Hierarchy:** Tuned for maximum legibility in glanceable lists, circle check-in notes, and quick reflections. Line height is purposefully open (`1.5` to `1.625`) to foster unhurried consumption.
- **Labels & Numbers:** Bold, crisp weights are reserved for micro-labels, member statuses, and numeric metrics like days completed and countdown meters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built mobile-first, prioritizing ergonomic one-handed use, instant touch access to daily micro-commitments, and friction-free social validation.

### Rhythm & Grid Model
- **Base Grid:** An 8-point base spacing rhythm underpins all layout boundaries, while 4-point steps handle micro-alignments, tag paddings, and avatar overlap offsets.
- **Layout Architecture:** A single-column fluid card layout on mobile (`max-width: 480px`), bounded by dynamic safe margins (`1rem` to `1.25rem`). Tablet layouts constrain circle feeds to a centered multi-column container (`max-width: 768px`) to preserve intimate focus.
- **Thumb Zone Design:** Primary interactive anchors (Check-in trigger, 'Toque de Aliento' quick-sheet, circle switcher) reside strictly within the lower 40% of the screen viewport.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **Tonal Layering with Radiant Soft Ambient Casts**. Deep drop shadows and rigid borders are omitted in favor of clean, organic levels:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Base surface (`#F8FAFC`), entirely non-elevated.
- **Level 1 (Habit & Circle Cards):** Pure background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a whisper outline (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`) and a diffused, low-opacity shadow (`0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`).
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Modal Sheets):** Elevated interactive components with warmth-tinted ambient glow:
  - Default Elevated: `0px 10px 30px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
  - Streak / Fire Accent Elevation: `0px 10px 28px -6px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.22)`
  - Completed Emerald State: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.20)`
- **Level 3 (Tactile Press):** Check-in buttons slightly compress inward (`scale(0.98)`), diminishing shadow spread for an authentic physical push response.

## Shapes

The interface utilizes a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape configuration, creating welcoming silhouettes that avoid geometric stiffness without drifting into childish proportions.

- **Primary Cards & Modals:** `1rem` (16px) to `1.5rem` (24px) border-radii provide a smooth frame for habit details and group member rosters.
- **Buttons & Interactive Indicators:** Action buttons, check-in chips, and streak badges adopt continuous pill silhouettes (`9999px`) for touch-inviting, friendly tactile boundaries.
- **Avatars & Streak Emblems:** Perfectly circular containers (`50%`), reinforced by concentric micro-rings when an active status or fresh streak is in effect.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Check-in Action Button:** Full-width or oversized tactile pill (`height: 3.5rem`). Uses a gradient blend from `#059669` to `#10B981` with white bold text. Upon tap, transitions to completed state with an organic pulse and checkmark animation.
- **Supportive Secondary:** Outlined or soft tinted pill (`rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.1)` with `#059669` label), providing quiet contrast for non-urgent tasks.
- **Nudge Action ('Aliento'):** Pill button tinted with translucent coral (`rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.12)`) featuring a flame or heart micro-icon.

### Circle Cards & Stacked Avatars
- **Circle Overview Card:** Houses 6 to 8 member avatars. Features circle name, daily completion meter (`x/8 completados`), and aggregate collective streak.
- **Stacked Avatars:** Overlapping circles with `-0.625rem` negative margins, framed by a `2px` surface border (`#FFFFFF`). Members who completed their habit display an emerald progress ring; pending members retain a muted slate border.

### Streak Indicators
- **Flame Badge:** High-energy pill featuring an animated warm coral flame icon paired with tabular numbers (`label-numeric-streak`). Uses an ambient flame glow (`0 0 16px rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.35)`).
- **Streak Freeze / Rest State:** Subdued icy amber tone (`#F59E0B` on soft sand backdrop), recognizing intentional rest without visual punishment.

### Lists & Habit Rows
- Single-item rows with generous vertical spacing (`padding: 1rem 1.25rem`). Left-aligned avatar with completion state, habit name, and time of execution; right-aligned 'Toque de Aliento' trigger for accountability peers.

### Inputs & Micro-Reflections
- Text areas for micro-check-ins (under 140 characters) use clean slate container surfaces (`#F1F5F9`) with no harsh stroke. Focus states produce a subtle `1.5px` emerald ring.

### Modals & Bottom Sheets ('Toque de Aliento')
- Detached bottom-sheet card with `1.5rem` rounded corners. Contains quick-reaction chips: "¡Tú puedes!", "¡Orgulloso de ti!", "¡Vamos por la racha!", and haptic-enabled customized nudges that deliver immediate micro-vibrations to the recipient.
